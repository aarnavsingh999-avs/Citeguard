# Fee Schedule, Payment Deadlines, and Refund Policy

**Document Code:** FIN-REF-2024-V3  
**Governing Body:** Office of Financial Affairs & Directorate of Student Accounts  
**Effective Date:** Academic Session 2024–2025  
**Applies to:** All Enrolled Students and Prospective Applicants  

---

## Section 2.1: Tuition Fee Payment Timelines and Late Fee Slabs
1. **Semester Fee Due Dates:** Tuition and institutional fees for each academic semester must be remitted on or before the specified due dates:
   - **Odd Semester (Autumn):** July 15 of every calendar year.
   - **Even Semester (Spring):** December 20 of every calendar year.
2. **Grace Period:** A grace period of **5 working days** is permitted without financial penalty.
3. **Late Fee Penalty Schedule:**
   - **Days 1 to 7 after grace period:** Late fine of **Rs. 500 per calendar day**.
   - **Days 8 to 14 after grace period:** Late fine of **Rs. 1,000 per calendar day**.
   - **Exceeding 14 days past grace period:** If the total semester dues remain unpaid after 14 days past grace period, student ERP access and course registration shall be automatically suspended. Reinstatement requires approval from the Registrar and payment of an administrative reactivation fee of **Rs. 5,000** in addition to accrued late fines.

---

## Section 2.2: Admission Cancellation and Tuition Fee Refund Slabs
In accordance with University Grants Commission (UGC) guidelines on fee refunds for higher education institutions, the following refund schedule applies to students withdrawing from a program of study:
1. **100% Refund (Tier 1):**
   - If formal written notification of withdrawal is received **15 days or more before the formally notified last date of admission**.
   - Deduction: A maximum administrative processing fee not exceeding **Rs. 1,000** or 5% of tuition fee (whichever is lower) will be deducted.
2. **90% Refund (Tier 2):**
   - If formal notification of withdrawal is received **less than 15 days before the formally notified last date of admission**.
3. **80% Refund (Tier 3):**
   - If formal notification of withdrawal is received **15 days or less after the formally notified last date of admission**.
4. **50% Refund (Tier 4):**
   - If formal notification of withdrawal is received **between 16 days and 30 days after the formally notified last date of admission**.
5. **0% Refund (Tier 5 - No Refund):**
   - If formal notification of withdrawal is received **more than 30 days after the formally notified last date of admission**. No tuition fee or development fee will be refunded under any circumstances.
6. **Processing Time:** All eligible refunds will be audited and disbursed directly to the student’s bank account on record within **30 working days** from the date of submission of the completed No-Dues Clearance Form.

## Section 2.3: Institutional Caution Deposit and Security Clearance
1. An Institutional Caution Deposit of **Rs. 20,000** is collected at the time of admission.
2. The caution deposit is completely **refundable** upon completion of the degree program or formal withdrawal from the university, subject to clearance of all university dues.
3. **Clearance Process:** The student must obtain clearance signatures from:
   - Department Laboratory In-Charge
   - Central Library
   - Chief Hostel Warden
   - Sports Complex & Gymnasium
   - IT Services Helpdesk
4. **Settlement Timeline:** Once the institutional clearance certificate is submitted to the Accounts Division, the net caution deposit (after adjusting any library fines or breakage damages) shall be credited via NEFT/RTGS within **45 business days**.

---

## Section 2.4: Installment Facilities and Merit Scholarships
1. **Fee Installment Plans:** Students experiencing documented financial hardship may petition the Financial Aid Committee for fee splitting. A maximum of **two installments** (60% at registration, 40% before mid-semester examinations) may be sanctioned with a non-refundable installment processing fee of **Rs. 2,500**.
2. **Merit Scholarship Adjustments:**
   - Merit scholarships (Dean’s List) are adjusted exclusively against tuition fee in subsequent semesters.
   - A student must maintain a minimum CGPA of **8.50** with zero backlogs and no disciplinary reprimands to retain scholarship eligibility across semesters.
