# Central Library Rules and IT Infrastructure Guidelines

**Document Code:** LIB-IT-2024-V2  
**Governing Body:** University Library Committee & Chief Information Officer (IT Services)  
**Effective Date:** August 15, 2024  
**Applies to:** All Registered Students, Faculty, and University Staff  

---

## Section 4.1: Library Circulation, Loan Quotas, and Renewals
1. **Circulation Privileges and Limits:**
   - **Undergraduate Students (B.Tech, BCA):** Maximum of **4 books** for a borrowing tenure of **14 calendar days**.
   - **Postgraduate Students (M.Tech, MCA, MBA):** Maximum of **6 books** for a borrowing tenure of **28 calendar days**.
   - **Doctoral Scholars (Ph.D.):** Maximum of **10 books** for a borrowing tenure of **60 calendar days**.
2. **Renewal Protocol:**
   - Borrowed items may be renewed online through the Library OPAC portal up to a maximum of **2 consecutive times**, provided no active reservation/hold has been placed by another patron.
   - Reference books, rare manuscripts, and current periodicals cannot be borrowed for home issue and are restricted to reading room consultation only.

---

## Section 4.2: Overdue Fines and Lost Book Penalties
1. **Daily Overdue Fine Structure:**
   - **First 7 overdue days:** Fine of **Rs. 5 per book per day**.
   - **8th day onwards:** Elevated fine of **Rs. 10 per book per day**.
   - Borrowing privileges are suspended automatically if outstanding fines exceed Rs. 200.
2. **Lost or Defaced Library Materials:**
   - If a borrowed book is lost, mutilated, or returned with missing pages, the borrower must:
     a) Replace the book with the latest available publisher edition of the same title, OR
     b) Pay **double the current market price** of the book, in addition to an administrative processing surcharge of **Rs. 200 per volume**.
3. **Semester No-Dues:** Clear library dues clearance is mandatory before receiving semester grade cards or degree certificates.

---

## Section 4.3: Campus Wi-Fi and Network Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
1. **Network Authentication and Device Limits:**
   - Every student is assigned a unique 802.1X secure Wi-Fi credential linked to their institutional ID.
   - A student is permitted to register a maximum of **3 personal devices** (e.g., laptop, smartphone, tablet) on the campus wireless network.
   - Account credentials must remain confidential; sharing credentials with external parties or fellow students is a direct violation of IT policy.
2. **Prohibited Network Activities:**
   - Engaging in Peer-to-Peer (P2P) torrent downloads, unauthorized network scanning, running rogue DHCP servers, setting up personal wireless routers, or cryptocurrency mining is strictly forbidden.
   - The Central IT Firewall automatically monitors, filters, and logs malicious traffic patterns.
3. **Disciplinary Sanctions for IT Violations:**
   - First violation results in an immediate **30-day MAC address ban** from the university network.
   - Repeated offenses result in referral to the Proctorial Board with possible suspension of campus IT and computer lab access for one semester.

---

## Section 4.4: Computer Laboratories and Printing Services
1. **Complimentary Semester Printing Quota:**
   - Each registered student receives an annual digital printing allowance of **150 complimentary black-and-white pages per semester** at the Central Computing Facility (CCF).
2. **Supplemental Printing Charges:**
   - Additional black-and-white printing is billed at **Rs. 2 per page**.
   - Color printing is billed at **Rs. 10 per page**, charged against the student digital campus wallet.
3. **IT Helpdesk Contact and Ticketing:**
   - Official IT Support Portal: `https://helpdesk.campus.edu`
   - Email: `helpdesk@campus.edu`
   - Central Campus Extension: Ext. 4400 (Operating hours: 09:00 AM to 06:00 PM, Monday through Saturday).
