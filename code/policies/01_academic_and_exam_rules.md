# Academic and Examination Regulations Handbook

**Document Code:** ACAD-REG-2024-V2  
**Governing Body:** Office of the Dean of Academic Affairs & Controller of Examinations  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2024  
**Applies to:** All Undergraduate (B.Tech, BCA), Postgraduate (M.Tech, MCA), and Doctoral Candidates  

---

## Section 1.1: Mandatory Attendance Requirements and Shortage Rules
1. Every registered student is required to maintain a minimum attendance of **75%** in each registered course (theory, tutorial, and practical sessions separately) during any semester.
2. Attendance is computed from the date of commencement of classes until the last instructional working day notified in the Academic Calendar.
3. **Medical Leave Condonation:** If a student fails to meet the 75% threshold due to severe medical hospitalization, prolonged illness, or representing the university in recognized inter-university sports/cultural/academic competitions:
   - A relaxation of up to **10%** may be sanctioned by the Dean of Academic Affairs, bringing the absolute minimum required attendance down to **65.0%**.
   - The student or their guardian must submit a formal condonation request along with valid medical documentation (hospital discharge summary, doctor's prescription, and medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner) to the Student Affairs Office within **5 working days** of returning to campus. Submissions past 5 working days will be summarily rejected.
4. **Debarment (Debarred List):** Any student whose cumulative attendance in a course falls below **65.0%** under any circumstance (including medical grounds), or below **75.0%** without approved condonation, shall be strictly **Debarred ('F' grade)** from appearing in the End-Semester Examination of that course. Such students must re-register for the course as a fresh backlog during the subsequent regular or summer semester.

## Section 1.2: Grading System and Academic Performance Indices
1. The university adopts a **10-point Letter Grading Scale** reflecting student mastery:
   - **O (Outstanding):** Grade Point 10.0 (Marks >= 90%)
   - **A+ (Excellent):** Grade Point 9.0 (Marks 80% - 89%)
   - **A (Very Good):** Grade Point 8.0 (Marks 70% - 79%)
   - **B+ (Good):** Grade Point 7.0 (Marks 60% - 69%)
   - **B (Above Average):** Grade Point 6.0 (Marks 50% - 59%)
   - **C (Average):** Grade Point 5.0 (Marks 45% - 49%)
   - **P (Pass):** Grade Point 4.0 (Marks 40% - 44%)
   - **F (Fail):** Grade Point 0.0 (Marks < 40%)
   - **I (Incomplete):** Assigned when examination is missed due to sanctioned emergency.
2. **Minimum Passing Criterion:** A candidate must secure at least a **Grade 'P' (40% aggregate marks)** in each course (with a minimum of 35% in continuous internal evaluation and 35% in end-semester evaluation) to earn course credits.
3. **SGPA & CGPA Calculation:**
   - **Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA):** SGPA = Sum(C_i * G_i) / Sum(C_i), where C_i is the credit of the i-th course and G_i is the grade point earned.
   - **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):** Calculated cumulatively across all registered semesters.
4. **Degree Classification:**
   - CGPA >= 8.50 with no backlogs in any semester: First Class with Distinction.
   - CGPA >= 6.50 and < 8.50: First Class.
   - CGPA >= 5.00 and < 6.50: Second Class.
   - Minimum CGPA for award of B.Tech Degree is **5.00**.

## Section 1.3: Backlog Examinations and Supplementary Terms
1. A student obtaining an 'F' grade in any course must clear the backlog through one of the following mechanisms:
   - **Supplementary / Remedial Examination:** Conducted immediately after summer recess for odd and even semester courses. A student can register for a maximum of **3 backlog courses** during a supplementary session.
   - **Summer Semester Re-Registration:** Offered for core failed courses; involves 6 weeks of intensive contact classes and payment of Rs. 6,500 per credit.
2. The maximum grade attainable in a Supplementary Examination is capped at **'B+' (Grade Point 7.0)** on the transcript, regardless of the raw score obtained.
3. **Maximum Academic Span:** A student enrolled in a 4-year undergraduate degree program (B.Tech) must complete all graduation requirements within a maximum tenure of **6 academic years** (12 consecutive semesters) from the date of initial admission. Failure to clear all requirements within 6 years will lead to termination of student registration.

---

## Section 1.4: Examination Grievance, Scrutiny, and Re-Evaluation
1. **Answer Script Inspection:** Students dissatisfied with their end-semester evaluation may apply for physical inspection / photocopies of evaluated answer scripts within **7 days** of declaration of results by submitting Form EX-04 and paying a verification fee of **Rs. 500 per course**.
2. **Re-Evaluation Application:**
   - A student may apply for formal re-evaluation of answer scripts within **15 calendar days** from the date of publication of official results.
   - The fee for formal re-evaluation is **Rs. 1,500 per subject**.
   - If the re-evaluation results in a score delta of +15% or higher over the original score, the entire re-evaluation fee (Rs. 1,500) shall be refunded to the student's registered bank account.
   - The revised score (higher or lower) awarded by the independent external evaluator shall be treated as final and binding on the student.

---

## Section 1.5: Academic Malpractice and Disciplinary Penalties
1. Any candidate found possessing unauthorized materials (mobile phones, smart watches, cheat sheets, written notes, programmable calculators) inside the examination hall shall be booked under **Unfair Means (UFM)**.
2. **Category A Malpractice (Possession of unauthorized material without copying):** Cancellation of the specific examination paper and award of Grade 'F' for that course.
3. **Category B Malpractice (Copying, receiving external assistance, or impersonation):**
   - Immediate cancellation of all registered examinations for the ongoing semester (all courses marked 'F').
   - Suspension from the university for one full academic semester.
   - Formal disciplinary reprimand noted in student's conduct record.
4. The Central Malpractice Inquiry Committee (CMIC) convenes within 10 days of exam completion to conduct hearings and pronounce final decisions.
