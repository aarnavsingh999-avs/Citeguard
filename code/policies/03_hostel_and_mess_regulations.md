# Residential Hostel Life and Dining Regulations

**Document Code:** HOST-REG-2024-V4  
**Governing Body:** Council of Wardens & Directorate of Student Housing  
**Effective Date:** Fall Semester 2024  
**Applies to:** All Campus Hostel Residents and Dining Facility Users  

---

## Section 3.1: Hostel Curfew Hours and Gate Entry Regulations
1. **Regular Curfew Timings:**
   - All campus resident students must enter the university campus premises by **10:00 PM** on weekdays (Monday through Friday).
   - Entry into respective hostel blocks closes strictly at **10:30 PM**.
2. **Weekend Relaxation:** On Saturdays, Sundays, and gazetted national holidays, campus entry is relaxed until **11:00 PM**, and hostel block entry closes at **11:30 PM**.
3. **Late Entry Violations:**
   - **First Infraction:** Biometric gate recording, automated SMS notification dispatched to parent/guardian, and formal written reprimand.
   - **Second Infraction:** Monetary fine of **Rs. 1,000** and mandatory meeting with the Associate Dean of Student Housing.
   - **Third Infraction:** Suspension of hostel accommodation privileges for the remainder of the semester; student must vacate the hostel premises within 48 hours.

---

## Section 3.2: Night Out and Leave Pass Approval Protocol
1. A student wishing to stay overnight outside the campus must apply for a **Night Out Pass** through the Student ERP Portal at least **24 hours in advance**.
2. **Parental Verification:**
   - The ERP system automatically sends an OTP/approval link via SMS and email to the registered mobile number of the student’s parent or legal guardian.
   - The pass is generated only after parental consent is confirmed digitally.
3. **Duration & Limits:** Students are permitted a maximum of **4 overnight leaves per calendar month** during regular academic sessions, excluding institutional vacation periods.
4. **Emergency Returns:** Any unannounced return between 11:30 PM and 6:00 AM requires authorization from the Chief Warden on duty.

---

## Section 3.3: Prohibited Items and Electrical Appliance Restrictions
1. **Permitted Electronic Items:** Laptops, tablets, mobile phones, basic table lamps, and portable chargers are strictly permitted.
2. **Prohibited Electrical Appliances:**
   - High-wattage electrical appliances including **electric kettles, induction cookers, room heaters, immersion heating rods, toasters, and electric irons** are strictly banned in student rooms due to electrical safety and fire regulations.
3. **Search & Confiscation Penalties:**
   - Designated hostel wardens and security personnel conduct periodic room safety inspections.
   - Any prohibited electrical item found in student custody will be confiscated permanently for the academic session.
   - A disciplinary safety fine of **Rs. 2,500** shall be levied per prohibited appliance found, payable through the student fee account within 7 days.
4. **Contraband Substances:** Consumption, possession, or distribution of alcoholic beverages, tobacco, vaping devices, or narcotics is punishable with immediate expulsion from the hostel and referral to the University Disciplinary Board for rustication.

---

## Section 3.4: Dining Timings and Mess Fee Rebate Policy
1. **Daily Mess Schedule:**
   - **Breakfast:** 07:30 AM to 09:30 AM (Sundays: 08:00 AM to 10:00 AM)
   - **Lunch:** 12:15 PM to 02:15 PM
   - **Evening Snacks & Tea:** 05:00 PM to 06:15 PM
   - **Dinner:** 07:45 PM to 09:45 PM
2. **Mess Rebate Eligibility:**
   - A student residing away from campus is eligible for a mess fee rebate provided the absence is for a minimum of **4 consecutive days**.
   - Prior Intimation: The mess rebate application must be submitted on the Dining Services ERP at least **48 hours prior** to the commencement of leave. Retroactive rebate claims are not entertained.
3. **Rebate Quantum:** An approved rebate is credited at the fixed rate of **Rs. 120 per day** and adjusted against the subsequent semester’s mess dues invoice.

---

## Section 3.5: Anti-Ragging Policy and Zero Tolerance Mandate
1. In compliance with the Hon’ble Supreme Court of India directives and UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations, the university enforces **Zero Tolerance** towards ragging in any form (physical, verbal, psychological, or digital).
2. Any student found engaging in, abetting, or conspiring in acts of ragging shall face immediate suspension from the university, forfeiture of hostel seat, and the immediate lodging of a **First Information Report (FIR)** with the local police under relevant penal laws.
